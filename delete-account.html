What gets deleted, and what's retained

Private records — expenses, income, budgets, categories, and lend/borrow records that only you can see are permanently deleted.

Shared records — if you've split a bill or shared a loan with someone else, your participation is removed, but the record itself is retained for the remaining participant so their history and balances stay accurate. No new data about you is added after deletion; only what was already recorded (e.g. a past repayment) may remain, consistent with our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

Settlements — confirmed repayment history tied to shared records is preserved for the remaining participant so their balances stay correct; it is not restored or altered after your account is deleted.
