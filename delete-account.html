Delete your Tuvi account

You can permanently delete your Tuvi account and the personal data associated with it from this page — no need to have the app installed.

Step 1 of 3

Sign in to identify your account

Sign in with the same Google account you use in Tuvi. We use this only to find your account — not to see your financial data.

Step 2 of 3

Confirm deletion

Clicking the button below will immediately, permanently:

  • Delete your private expenses, income, budgets, categories, and solo lend/borrow records.
  • Remove you from any shared records (split bills, shared loans) — the remaining participant keeps their copy and repayment history, as described below.
  • Delete your Tuvi sign-in account.

This happens immediately and cannot be undone. There is no review period — once you confirm, the deletion runs right away, the same way it would if you did it from inside the app.

Account deleted

Your Tuvi account and private data have been permanently deleted. Keep this reference for your records:

If you signed in to the app on a device, it will sign you out automatically the next time it tries to sync.

Questions? Contact support.tuvi@gmail.com and mention this reference.

What gets deleted, and what's retained

Private records — expenses, income, budgets, categories, and lend/borrow records that only you can see are permanently deleted.

Shared records — if you've split a bill or shared a loan with someone else, your participation is removed, but the record itself is retained for the remaining participant so their history and balances stay accurate. No new data about you is added after deletion; only what was already recorded (e.g. a past repayment) may remain, consistent with our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

Settlements — confirmed repayment history tied to shared records is preserved for the remaining participant so their balances stay correct; it is not restored or altered after your account is deleted.

If you'd rather request deletion by email instead of this form, contact support.tuvi@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Tuvi account.
